⚠️ Warning: this guide contains serious errors

Do not reproduce this tutorial. It makes several dangerous or scientifically unfounded claims:

  1. Cardboard on the cheekbones: it does not protect your bones from ultrasound at all.
  2. Device noise: not a reliable indicator of safety or bone proximity.
  3. Aloe vera gel: not necessarily a substitute for a proper coupling gel.
  4. 15 minutes and 70%: cannot be considered safe without knowing the exact device and its intended medical use.
  5. Lymphatic drainage: does not eliminate destroyed fat.
  6. Diet: cutting carbs after a session is not a scientifically established way to improve results.

Most important: focused ultrasound or other ultrasound devices can cause thermal injuries and tissue damage. The face contains nerves, blood vessels, hearing structures and very sensitive eyes. One uneventful personal experience proves nothing about safety. Home use on the face, especially around cheekbones and eyes, is strongly discouraged.
